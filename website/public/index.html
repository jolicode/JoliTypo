JoliTypo demo

A PHP micro-typography fixer: it fixes the typographic errors of your HTML or text by applying a set of rules. Pick a locale and some fixers, paste your content, and see the result instantly. Everything runs in your browser, thanks to PHP compiled to WebAssembly.
